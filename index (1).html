01 / The idea

Useful tools, without unnecessary barriers.

ALTech PDF is an independent software project, not a claim of a large company or a promise that every planned feature is already finished. The goal is to make common PDF operations accessible through a clear, easy-to-use interface.

↗

Easy to use

Find a tool, choose a document, make the change, and save the result. The interface should help users complete a task without unnecessary steps.

○

No account required

The intended experience does not require users to create an ALTech PDF account or log in to use the available tools.

◇

Free access

The project aims to keep its available PDF tools free to use, without hidden charges for ordinary access to those features.

↻

Improved over time

Features are tested and refined over time. Availability and behavior can differ by app version, and planned features are not presented as completed features.

02 / Privacy and file handling

Your documents stay on your device.

ALTech PDF is designed to process PDF documents locally on the device. The app's PDF-processing flow uses on-device libraries rather than sending documents to an ALTech PDF processing server.

What this means: the PDF files you choose for processing are not uploaded to ALTech PDF servers, and the project does not receive or keep copies of those documents through a remote PDF-processing service.

This statement is about the handling of document contents. It does not mean the app or any third-party services necessarily collect no technical information. Device, usage, analytics, or advertising data may be handled depending on the app version, platform settings, and integrated services. See the separate Privacy Policy for details.

03 / A transparent model

Free to use. Intended to be supported by ads.

The planned sustainability model for ALTech PDF is advertising through Google AdMob. This is intended to help support maintenance and continued development while keeping the available tools free for users.

No paid account

The core experience is designed without a mandatory account or login.

No hidden access fee

The goal is to fund the project through advertising rather than charging users just to access the available tools.

Advertising availability and implementation may vary by release. Third-party advertising services may process data as described in the Privacy Policy and their own disclosures.

04 / Development

Built carefully, one improvement at a time.

ALTech PDF is an ongoing project. Development priorities are reliability, a straightforward interface, and making the available PDF tools work as expected before adding unnecessary complexity.

Make the basics reliable

Test existing tools, improve file handling, and fix issues without breaking unrelated features.

Keep the experience simple

Prioritize clear navigation and useful functionality over features that add complexity without enough value.

Add features responsibly

New tools, including scanning, may be considered for future updates after they are implemented and tested.

This page describes the project's principles and direction. It is not a guarantee that every possible tool or future feature is available in the current release.
